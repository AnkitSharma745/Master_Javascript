# Mock interview scripts

Use a partner or record yourself. Keep prompts separate from answers. The interviewer should change one assumption and ask a follow-up after each initial response. Do not interrupt every hesitation; observe how reasoning recovers.

## Mock A: language and coding, 45 minutes

5 minutes clarify experience/goal; 8 minutes Q04/Q05; 7 minutes Q10/Q11; 20 minutes P02 or P03; 5 minutes candidate explanation and feedback. Follow-ups: parameter rebinding, typeof TDZ, nested microtasks, duplicate search targets, Unicode boundaries. Evaluate correctness, mechanism, invariant, edge tests, complexity, and spoken clarity.

## Mock B: Angular/frontend, 50 minutes

10 minutes Q19/Q20; 10 minutes debug a failed search workflow; 25 minutes Case 1 or 5; 5 minutes an ADR defense. Change the assumptions: tenant switches mid-request, a user loses permission, or offline editing is requested. Ask which APIs are valid in Angular 16 and which tests were actually run.

## Mock C: Node/full-stack, 50 minutes

10 minutes Q23/Q24; 10 minutes trace malformed and duplicate HTTP requests in the issue board; 20 minutes Q30/Q31 design; 5 minutes Q25; 5 minutes discuss one limitation and a migration plan. Ask what survives restart, what happens after timeout, and how queue duplicates affect side effects.

## Mock D: senior engineering and behavioral, 45 minutes

10 minutes describe a real incident using context/responsibility/actions/result/learning; 20 minutes Q22 plus a changed constraint; 10 minutes Q17/Q32; 5 minutes feedback. Do not invent performance numbers, users, or personal contributions. Probe a rejected alternative, communication with a teammate, and a point where the candidate changed their mind based on evidence.

## Feedback and retest

For each response record one correct mechanism, one unsupported claim, one missed boundary, and one clearer phrasing. Rate the seven dimensions separately. Correct the underlying concept, then answer an unfamiliar follow-up in a later session. Repeated fluent recitation of the same prompt is insufficient.
