# Role-specific interview tracks

Each track links to authored lessons and questions. Complete prerequisites even when a role's title sounds advanced. The recommended order follows the master curriculum; communication drills from IV01 can begin after F01/JS02, while its later scenarios should be used when their technical lessons are reached. These are preparation choices, not claims about one employer's hiring process.
